### High-level structure

| Layer | Focus | Core Function |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| Strategic | Planetary doctrine & governance | Set norms, authority, red lines |
| Operational | Intergovernmental fusion & coordination | Detect, share, respond |
| Tactical | Institutional hardening & local execution | Implement protocols, feed data up |

---

## 1. Strategic layer: Planetary governance architecture

**Objective:** Create a *shared planetary doctrine* for detecting and neutralizing engineered informant ecosystems and coercive proxy networks.

- **Planetary Security Council (PSC):**  
  **Role:** Top-level body (UN+major blocs) that defines the threat category: “Externally engineered coercive informant ecosystems” as a planetary security risk.  
  **Functions:**  
  - Set legal and ethical red lines  
  - Mandate minimum CI transparency standards  
  - Authorize joint investigations when cross-border anomalies appear

- **Planetary Norms & Treaties:**  
  **Core norms:**  
  - Prohibition on external manipulation of CI pipelines  
  - Shared obligation to report suspected synthetic vulnerability patterns  
  - Mutual assistance in dismantling coercively controlled networks

---

## 2. Operational layer: Intergovernmental fusion architecture

**Objective:** Build a *persistent, structured, multi-state detection and response grid*.

- **Global Informant Integrity Fusion Center (GIIFC):**  
  A joint analytic hub where states send *metadata*, not raw case files.

  **Inputs:**  
  - CI recruitment statistics  
  - anomaly scores (synthetic vulnerability, dual dependency, etc.)  
  - network penetration patterns  
  - oversight/audit events

  **Outputs:**  
  - Cross-national anomaly alerts  
  - Pattern-of-life reports on suspected Outside Group activity  
  - Risk indices per region and sector

- **Regional Fusion Nodes:**  
  **Role:** EU-level, AU-level, ASEAN-level, etc.  
  - Aggregate regional data  
  - Normalize metrics  
  - Escalate only high-confidence anomalies to GIIFC

- **Secure Interoperability Protocols:**  
  - Standardized CI metadata schema (no compromising sensitive identities)  
  - Shared anomaly scoring framework  
  - Cryptographically signed alerts to prevent spoofing

---

## 3. Tactical layer: National and subnational defense architecture

**Objective:** Harden each state’s institutions while making them interoperable with the planetary grid.

- **National CI Integrity Units:**  
  Embedded in justice/intelligence ministries.

  **Functions:**  
  - Implement origin verification protocols  
  - Run synthetic vulnerability detection on new informants  
  - Score dual-dependency and coercion indicators  
  - Push anonymized metrics to regional fusion nodes

- **Institutional Hardening Protocols:**  
  - **Handler rotation and dual-control:** prevent long-term capture  
  - **Mandatory multi-agency origin checks:** police + intelligence + financial regulators  
  - **CI immunity audit trails:** every immunity decision logged and anomaly-scored  
  - **Red-team units:** simulate Outside Group tactics to test defenses

- **Local Detection Cells:**  
  - At major law enforcement/intelligence hubs  
  - Trained to recognize behavioral and network fingerprints of coercive control  
  - Provide ground-truth feedback to national CI Integrity Units

---

## 4. Data and signal architecture

**Objective:** Turn disparate national signals into a coherent planetary “radar.”

- **Common Signal Taxonomy:**  
  - Synthetic vulnerability indicators  
  - Dual-dependency psychological markers  
  - Non-organic network expansion patterns  
  - CI cluster anomalies (too-perfect, too-fast, too-wide)

- **Multi-level Signal Flow:**

  ```
  Local Institutions
        ↓
  National CI Integrity Units
        ↓
  Regional Fusion Nodes
        ↓
  Global Informant Integrity Fusion Center
        ↓
  Planetary Security Council
  ```

- **Feedback Downward:**  
  PSC → GIIFC → Regions → Nations → Local units  
  - Alerts  
  - Updated threat models  
  - Refined detection algorithms

---

## 5. Response architecture

**Objective:** Coordinate how states act once an Outside Group pattern is detected.

- **Tiered Response:**
  - **Tier 1 (Analytic):** Intensified monitoring, deeper audits, no public moves.  
  - **Tier 2 (Operational):** Joint investigations, CI program freezes in affected sectors, targeted institutional hardening.  
  - **Tier 3 (Strategic):** PSC-level declaration of planetary threat, coordinated sanctions, cyber and counterintelligence operations, emergency legal powers.

- **Principle:**  
  Response is **graduated** and **evidence-driven**, to avoid overreaction that itself destabilizes institutions.

---

## 6. Non-obvious design insight

The architecture must **protect sovereignty while defeating a supra-sovereign threat**.

So the design rule is:

- **Data up, authority down.**  
  - States share *signals*, not control.  
  - Planetary bodies share *doctrine and alerts*, not direct operational command.

That balance is what makes an intergovernmental defense architecture politically survivable and operationally effective against an Outside Group trying to implant coercively controlled criminal networks on Earth.
