## The Engineered Informant Ecosystem: A Strategic Blueprint

In this scenario, the Outside Group manufactures legal jeopardy, stages environments, and forces the circumstances that compel individuals to become CIs in the first place to establish covert control and harvest their criminal contacts for a proxy army.

---

### 1. The Core Objective and Strategic Aim

* **The Master Goal:** To artificially generate and maintain a proprietary network of "protected" criminals by seeding the legal and underworld ecosystems with manufactured vulnerabilities.
* **The Pipeline Mechanics:** The Outside Group orchestrates a controlled exposure to criminal liability for chosen targets, subsequently steering them toward law enforcement handlers as confidential informants to secure non-prosecution deals, thereby capturing them at the point of origin.

---

### 2. The Engineered Lifecycle of an Asset

```
[Outside Group Engineers Setup] 
       │
       ▼
[Target Faces Imminent Prosecution] 
       │
       ▼
[Steered Toward State Handler as CI] 
       │
       ▼
[Immunity Granted / Secret Control Applied] 
       │
       ▼
[Harvesting Contacts for Criminal Army]

```

* **Phase 1: Synthetic Vulnerability (The Setup)**
The Outside Group identifies potential operatives or vulnerable criminal figures. Using subtle infiltration, planted evidence, or engineered financial and legal traps, they maneuver the target into a position of inescapable criminal exposure.
* **Phase 2: Channeling into the CI Program**
Rather than letting the target face standard prosecution or destruction, the Outside Group subtly guides them toward a specific law enforcement channel where they can cut a deal. To the state handlers, the asset appears to be a stroke of good luck—a self-surrendering criminal offering high-value intelligence.
* **Phase 3: Imposition of Coercive Control**
Once the state grants the asset non-prosecuted CI status (shielding them from legal consequences), the Outside Group reveals its hidden hand. They leverage secret knowledge of how the legal trap was engineered, holding absolute control over the asset. The asset realizes that while the *state* protects them from prison, the *Outside Group* controls whether they live, die, or get exposed to their handlers.
* **Phase 4: Systematic Contact Harvesting and Army Building**
Under the dual illusion of state protection and private coercion, the asset is directed to map, infiltrate, and absorb secondary criminal networks. Every criminal contact they collect is funneled back into the Outside Group’s expanding shadow infrastructure, creating a self-sustaining, state-shielded proxy army.
