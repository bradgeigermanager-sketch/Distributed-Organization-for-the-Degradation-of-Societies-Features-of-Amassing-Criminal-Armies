Imagine a scenario involving a plea-bargain (or prior to judicial trial arrangement being reached to prevent charges being brought against a criminal) where a criminal was to avoid punishment by assisting with the collecting of criminals, and or the gathering of information on them by getting into contact with them, and / or attracting their attention. Additionally, because of whatever circumstances, this arrangement was essentially going to continue on an indefinite basis, so that bad actor, in exchange for not being imprisoned, was then to be essentially continuously involved in the collection of criminals, on behalf of the organization sponsoring that behavior.
